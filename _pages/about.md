Kevin Echavarria is a 2nd-year PhD student in Communication at the University of California, San Diego. He graduated with a Masters in Media, Culture, and Communication from New York University in 2025, after a 10+ year career as a trends forecaster and analyst in the advertising industry with the Chicago Humanities Festival, Omnicom Group's cultural consultancy sparks & honey, and the global headquarters of McCann Worldgroup. In both his academic and professional lives, Kevin has been interested in the networks and practices of cultural production, how institutions and corporate capital mainstream the avant garde, and how subaltern communities and actors resist commodification and reimagine their futures.

Currently, his research focuses on the critical political economy of the advertising and consulting industries and their work with social development projects, labor in the knowledge economy, and the emerging technologies of borders, geography, and spatialization in the Capitalocene.
